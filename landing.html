02 / Operational Methodology

The ConnectEM introduction lifecycle.

01

Discover & Match

Upload target accounts or search decision makers. ConnectEM scores warm paths (0–100) using relationship strength, recency, and partner credibility while preserving contact privacy.

02

Double Opt-In Introduction

Vendor provides context and a specific ask. ConnectEM formats a forwardable brief in the Partner's voice. The Partner initiates a "Check Interest" link; the Contact opts in with one click without creating an account.

03

Meeting Intel & Attribution

Parties receive a pre-meeting brief outlining relationship nuance and "what NOT to do." 24-hour post-meeting feedback captures pipeline stages, closing revenue, and updating partner trust scores.

03 / Core Principles

Non-negotiable principles of human trust.

[01]

Partners Control Their Contacts

Contacts are never sold or cold-emailed. Visibility defaults conservatively to "Ask Partner" or "Anonymous." Declining a request never penalizes the Partner.

[02]

Zero Friction for Contacts

Target contacts do not need an account. They receive a clean, forwardable preview from their trusted partner and decide in seconds whether to accept.

[03]

Revenue & Pipeline Measurement

We measure commercial outcomes (meetings held, pipeline generated, closed ARR), not vanity metrics or connection counts. Partners share in the real enterprise value created.
