Partner Network Agreement

1. Purpose and Scope

This Partner Network Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the terms governing the submission, qualification, and compensation of warm executive introductions provided by Partner to Enterprise buyers.

2. Introduction Standards & Permission

Partner represents and warrants that all introductions submitted through the Partner Workspace are permission-based, with prior consent obtained from the target executive.

3. Milestone Fee Schedule & Rewards

Points and reward compensation are earned upon verified operational milestones:
• Warm Introduction Verification: 200 points
• Completed Executive Discussion: 600 points
• Active Pipeline Opportunity: 1,000 points
• Executed Enterprise Contract: 5,000 points

4. Confidentiality & Mutual Non-Disclosure

Each party agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of executive contact details, commercial opportunities, and proprietary methodology.
