Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how ConnectEM handles information when you use the ConnectEM partner workspace and related services. ConnectEM provides tools for trusted business introductions, partner relationship management, email, and meeting scheduling.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use the service, ConnectEM may handle account and profile details; business contact and relationship information you enter or import; introduction requests and activity records; and email or meeting details used for workflows you initiate. Do not upload information you are not authorized to provide.

Google account data

If you connect Google, ConnectEM requests access to the Google account information and Gmail and Calendar features shown on the consent screen. The service uses this access to identify the connected account, display mailbox messages, send email you choose to send, and create calendar events for meetings you schedule.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that you request, for security, or as required by law. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build generalized AI models. ConnectEM does not disclose Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the requested feature, for security, with your consent, or as required by law. Email or meeting details may be processed by the AI provider you select when you explicitly request an AI feature. You can disconnect Google in the partner workspace; you can also revoke ConnectEM’s access in your Google Account settings.

AI and other service providers

If you configure an AI provider and use an AI feature, the relevant prompt and information you choose to process are sent to that provider to generate the requested result. Provider terms and privacy practices also apply. ConnectEM uses hosting, authentication, and database providers to operate the service. These providers process information on ConnectEM’s behalf and under their service terms.

Contact privacy and sharing

Partners control the contact information they add and the visibility choices available in the service. ConnectEM uses that information to provide partner relationship and introduction workflows. Information is shared with other users only as needed for a workflow, according to the selected visibility and approval settings, or as otherwise described in the service.

Retention and security

ConnectEM retains information while it is needed to provide the service, maintain account and business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Google access tokens and provider credentials are protected using encryption at rest in the hosted service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can disconnect Google, update information in the workspace, and contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes. Contact contact@connectem.ai for privacy requests.

Children and changes

ConnectEM is a business service and is not intended for children under 18. We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@connectem.ai.
